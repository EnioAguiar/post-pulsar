Short-form video isn't just a trend; it's the new language of the internet. Platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts are where audiences are spending their time. But as a content creator focused on in-depth articles, how do you tap into this wave without starting from scratch?

The answer is already in your archives. Your blog posts are the perfect source material for compelling short-form videos. This guide will show you how to systematically turn your written content into engaging videos that capture attention.

### Step 1: Mine Your Article for Video Gold

Not every part of your blog post is suitable for a 60-second video. You need to find the most potent, standalone ideas. Scour your article for:

*   **The Strongest Opinion:** What is the most controversial or passionate point you make?
*   **The "Aha!" Moment:** What is the single most surprising statistic, fact, or insight?
*   **The Quick How-To:** Is there a specific, actionable tip that you can demonstrate or explain quickly?
*   **The Listicle Item:** If your post is a list (e.g., "5 Ways to..."), each item is a potential video.

Choose ONE of these "golden nuggets" to be the entire focus of your video. Don't try to cram the whole article into 60 seconds.

### Step 2: Script a Killer 3-Second Hook

In short-form video, you don't have five minutes to earn a viewer's attention—you have three seconds. Your hook is everything. A good hook poses a question, makes a bold statement, or challenges a common belief.

Here’s how to derive a hook from your blog post's nugget:

*   **If your nugget is a tip:** "Stop doing [Common Mistake]. Do this instead..."
*   **If your nugget is a stat:** "Did you know that [X]% of people struggle with [Problem]?"
*   **If your nugget is an opinion:** "Unpopular opinion: [Your Controversial Take]. Here's why..."

Write down 3-5 potential hooks and say them out loud. Pick the one that sounds the most intriguing.

### Step 3: Follow the Simple 3-Part Video Script

Once you have your hook and your core idea, the rest of the script falls into place. Use this simple structure:

1.  **The Hook (3 seconds):** The powerful opening you just crafted.
2.  **The Value (45 seconds):** Deliver the golden nugget. Explain the tip, break down the statistic, or justify your opinion. Keep your sentences short and your language simple. This is where you deliver the core value of the video.
3.  **The Call-to-Action (CTA) (10 seconds):** Tell the viewer what to do next. Don't just end your video. A good CTA encourages engagement or directs them to learn more.
    *   *Good CTAs:* "Follow for more tips on [Your Topic]" or "What do you think? Let me know in the comments."
    *   *Great CTA:* "This is just one tip from my full article on [Topic]. Click the link in my bio to read the rest."

### Step 4: Choose Your Visual Format

You don't need a professional film studio. Authenticity often performs better. Choose a simple format:

*   **Talking Head:** Just you, talking to the camera. It's direct and builds a personal connection.
*   **Text on Screen:** Use a trending audio clip and display your script as text on the screen. This is fast and effective.
*   **Screen Recording / B-Roll:** If you're explaining a process, record your screen. You can also use relevant stock footage (from sites like Pexels) with a voiceover.

### Step 5: Edit for a Short Attention Span

How you edit is as important as what you say. Keep the viewer engaged:

*   **Add Captions:** Most videos are watched without sound. Burned-in captions are non-negotiable.
*   **Use Quick Cuts:** Change the camera angle or zoom in and out every 3-5 seconds to keep the visual experience dynamic.
*   **Highlight Keywords:** Make important words pop on screen as you say them.
*   **Add Background Music:** Use a trending (but not distracting) audio track to increase your video's reach.

### From Writer to Multimedia Creator

Repurposing your blog posts into videos isn't about creating more work; it's about making your existing work go further. By following this system, you can efficiently tap into the massive audience on video platforms.
