At PostPulsar, our mission has always been to destroy the "hell" of content repurposing. We started by turning your blog posts into high-quality social media content. Today, we're taking two massive leaps forward.

We're thrilled to announce two powerful new features, available now to all users:

1.  **YouTube Video Transcription:** Turn any YouTube video into a text-based article, ready to be "pulsed" into content for all your social networks.
2.  **AI Quote Image Generation:** Automatically create beautiful, shareable quote images from any article or text, complete with your branding.

Let's break down how you can use these features to save hours and create more engaging content than ever before.

### From Video to Viral: How YouTube Transcription Works

You've spent hours scripting, recording, and editing a video. Don't let that value stay locked on YouTube. Our new transcription feature turns your video's spoken words into a content goldmine.

**How it works:**

1.  **Paste a YouTube URL:** In the PostPulsar dashboard, simply paste the URL of the YouTube video you want to repurpose.
2.  **AI Transcription:** Our backend service gets to work. It extracts the audio, transcribes it using a powerful AI model (the same technology behind Whisper), and delivers a clean, text-based version of your video script.
3.  **Pulsar, as Usual:** With the text ready, you can now use the core PostPulsar engine to generate posts for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, and more.

This workflow is designed to be seamless. What was once a multi-hour task of manual transcription is now a two-click process.

**Pulse Cost:** This powerful feature costs just **2 Pulses** for the initial transcription, plus the standard 1 Pulse for each social media post you generate from it.

### Stop the Scroll: AI-Powered Image Generation

Static images get more engagement on platforms like Instagram and Facebook, but creating them is a time-consuming design task. Not anymore.

Our new Image Generation feature extracts impactful quotes from your content and turns them into stunning visuals automatically.

**How it works:**

1.  **Start from URL or Text:** You can either paste an article URL or enter text directly.
2.  **AI Quote Extraction:** Our AI model reads your content and identifies a short, powerful, and shareable quote.
3.  **Customize Your Template:** You're in control. Choose from multiple design templates, select your brand's highlight color, and even customize the font to match your aesthetic.
4.  **Generate & Publish:** With one click, a beautiful quote image is generated and ready to be published or downloaded.

This feature is perfect for creating professional-looking content for Instagram, Facebook, and even LinkedIn.

**Pulse Cost:** The cost is simple.

- **From Text:** 1 Pulse to extract the quote and generate the image.
- **From URL:** 2 Pulses (1 to scrape the article, 1 to generate the image).
